Two detectors, and a way to see why.

Check photos for signs of AI generation and documents for signs of digital editing. Every result shows what influenced it.

AI-generated image detection

Pick a photo from your gallery, camera, or downloads. A MobileNetV2 model estimates whether an AI generator made it.

  • Works on photos, screenshots, and shared images
  • Result shows a verdict and a confidence score
  • Adjustable sensitivity

Tampered document detection

Upload an image or PDF. Error Level Analysis and a CNN look for signs of digital splicing in scanned documents and receipts.

  • Accepts images and PDFs
  • Heatmap of areas with unusual compression
  • Adjustable sensitivity

  • “Why this result?”

    The app masks parts of the image and re-runs the model to show which regions pushed the verdict the most, in plain language.

  • Scan from anywhere

    Get a “Scan this screenshot?” prompt right after you take a screenshot, or share an image from Facebook or Messenger straight into the app.

  • History and PDF reports

    Every scan is saved to an encrypted history on your phone. Export a PDF report that includes the model version and its accuracy.

  • Tell us when it’s wrong

    Tap “This result is wrong” to send a small copy of that image to help improve the model. The app asks you to confirm first.

From suspicious to sorted in three steps.

  1. Pick or share

    Choose a photo or document, or share one to AI-ImageAD from another app.

  2. Analyze on your phone

    The models run on your device, so your image isn’t uploaded to be analyzed.

  3. Review the result

    Get a verdict, a confidence score, and a look at what influenced it.

Your photos and documents are personal.

Before you scan anything, the app explains what it accesses, why, and how it’s stored, then asks for your consent. You can decline, and you can withdraw consent later.

  • Email code on every sign-in

    A 6-digit code is sent to your email each time you log in with email or Google.

  • Alerts for new sign-ins

    You get an email whenever your account signs in, so you can spot activity that isn’t yours.

  • Lockout after failed attempts

    Five wrong passwords lock that email address for five minutes.

  • Encrypted history on your phone

    Your scan history stays on your device, encrypted with AES-256 (SQLCipher).

  • Nothing uploaded automatically

    Analysis runs on your device. An image is sent only if you tap “This result is wrong” and confirm.

Known limitations

  • Posed, well-lit portraits can score falsely high as AI-generated.
  • Newer generators, such as Midjourney v6 or Flux, may not be caught reliably.
  • Document analysis finds digital splicing artifacts. It can’t tell whether a document’s contents are true.

A result is a probability, not proof. Use it as one signal, not the final word.
